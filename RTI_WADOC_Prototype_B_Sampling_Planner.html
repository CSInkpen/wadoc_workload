Workload Study Sampling Planner Prototype – county data as office proxy

Adjust the stratification rules, draw a probability sample of offices, and see the effect on precision and burden. County characteristics come from ACS 2022, USDA RUCC 2023, and 2019–2020 Census and County Health Rankings compilations; DOC office, CCO, and caseload figures are simulated from population until DOC's frame is loaded.
